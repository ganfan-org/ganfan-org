Ganfan Journal

A simple and elegant journaling app that makes recording your life effortless and beautiful.

  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac
  • Price: Free — no ads, no in-app purchases
  • Languages: Simplified Chinese, English

Key Features​

Quick Capture​

Minimal interface, start writing instantly. Focus on your words, nothing else.

Mood Tracker​

Mark each day's mood with emojis. See how you feel over time.

iCloud Sync​

Your journals auto-sync across your iPhone and Mac through your own iCloud. Safe and reliable.

Fast Search​

Full-text search to easily find past entries.

Dark Mode​

System dark mode support for comfortable night writing.

Privacy First​

  • Data stored only in your own iCloud
  • No personal information collected
  • No third-party servers, no analytics, no ads

Read the full Privacy Policy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### iCloud sync isn't working

Please make sure all your devices are signed in with the same Apple ID and that iCloud Drive is enabled in Settings. Sync may take a few minutes. Switching the app to foreground and back can trigger a sync.

### Will I lose my data?

Your data is stored in your own iCloud. As long as your iCloud account is active, your data is safe. We recommend ensuring iCloud Backup is enabled.

### How do I change the language?

Open the app → Settings → Language, then select your preferred language.

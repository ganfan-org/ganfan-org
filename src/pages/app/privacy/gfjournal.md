# Ganfan Journal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

## Overview

Ganfan Journal ("the app") is designed to protect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Your journal entries belong to you and only you.

## Data Storage

All content you create in Ganfan Journal — including text, moods, and media — is stored **locally on your device** and synced through **Apple iCloud** using your personal iCloud account.

- We do not have access to your journal content
- We do not store your data on any third-party servers
- Your data is protected by Apple's iCloud security

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

- Personal information (name, email, etc.)
- Journal content or metadata
- Usage statistics or analytics
- Location data
- Contact lists
- Advertising identifiers

## Third-Party Services

The app uses the following system services provided by Apple:

- **iCloud / CloudKit**: For syncing your data across devices. Governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

We do not integrate with any other third-party services, SDKs, or analytics platforms.

## Data Deletion

Since we do not store your data, there is nothing to delete from our side. To remove your data:

1. Delete the app from your devices
2. Remove app data from your iCloud storage in Settings

## Children's Privacy

Ganfan Journal does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at [support@ganfan.org](mailto:support@ganfan.org).
